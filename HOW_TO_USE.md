# How to Use JobRight AI - Complete Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create Your First Profile

1. **Click the JobRight AI icon** in your Chrome toolbar
2. **Click "Manage Profiles"** → Opens Options page
3. **Click "Create New Profile"**

### Step 2: Fill in Your Information

**Required Fields:**
- ✅ Profile Name (e.g., "My Main Profile")
- ✅ Email
- ✅ First Name
- ✅ Last Name

**Recommended Fields:**
- Phone, Location, Country
- All work authorization questions
- All education fields
- Links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio)

### Step 3: Set Profile as Active

1. Go back to the **popup** (click extension icon)
2. **Select your profile** from the dropdown
3. Green checkmark appears: "✓ Active: [Profile Name]"

### Step 4: Apply to Jobs!

1. Navigate to any job application
2. Click **"Scan Current Page"** in the popup
3. Purple overlay appears with suggestions
4. Click **"Accept All Safe Fields"** or review individually

## 📝 Answer Bank (Pre-written Answers)

### What Is It?
Pre-write answers to common open-ended questions. When the extension detects similar questions, it auto-fills your prepared answer!

### Supported Questions:
1. **Why are you interested in this role?**
2. **Why do you want to work here?**
3. **Tell us about yourself**
4. **What are your greatest strengths?**
5. **What is a weakness/area of improvement?**
6. **Describe a time you led a team**
7. **Describe a challenge you overcame**
8. **What is your greatest achievement?**
9. **Where do you see yourself in 5 years?**
10. **Anything else you'd like us to know?**

### Tips for Writing Good Answers:
- Keep answers 100-300 words
- Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Include specific numbers/metrics
- Be authentic - you'll need to elaborate in interviews!

## 🤖 AI Features (Requires Perplexity API)

### Setting Up AI

1. Go to **Options** → **AI Settings** tab
2. Enter your **Perplexity API Key**
3. Click "Test" to verify it works
4. Check "Enable AI-powered features"
5. Select your preferred model:
   - **Sonar** - Fast & cheap
   - **Sonar Pro** - Better quality (recommended)
   - **Sonar Reasoning** - Best for complex tasks

### What AI Does

1. **Smart Answer Generation** - Generates personalized answers using your resume
2. **Question Understanding** - Better matches questions to your profile
3. **Cover Letter Ideas** - Helps draft compelling content

### Using Resume with AI

1. Go to **Profile** → **Resume (Text Version)**
2. Paste your ENTIRE resume as plain text
3. The more detail, the better the AI answers!

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### "No form fields found"
1. Make sure you're on the form page (not the job listing)
2. Some sites use unusual form structures
3. Try clicking directly into a field and scanning again

### "Content script not loaded"
1. Reload the page
2. Reload the extension at `chrome://extensions/`
3. Try on an HTTP/HTTPS page (not file:// URLs)

### Buttons not working
1. Clear browser cache
2. Reload extension
3. Check console (F12) for errors

### AI not working
1. Check API key is entered correctly
2. Click "Test" to verify
3. Make sure AI is enabled (checkbox)
4. Check your Perplexity API credits

## 🆕 New Features

### 1. Missing Fields Detector
When you scan a page, click the **"Missing"** tab to see:
- Which questions couldn't be auto-filled
- What profile fields you should add
- Click "Open Profile" to add them

### 2. Learn from Corrections
When you **edit a suggestion** before filling:
1. Click on the answer text in the overlay
2. Edit it to your preferred answer
3. Click the item to fill
4. The extension remembers your preference!

Next time you see a similar question, it uses your learned answer with 98% confidence.

### 3. LinkedIn Import
1. Click **"Import from LinkedIn"** in the profile form
2. Follow the steps to export your LinkedIn profile as PDF
3. Copy the text and paste it in the Resume field
4. Click **"Parse Resume Text"** to auto-fill!

### 4. Resume Parser (AI-powered)
1. Paste your resume text in the **"Resume (Text Version)"** field
2. Click **"Parse Resume Text"**
3. AI extracts and auto-fills:
   - Name, email, phone
   - Current title, experience
   - Education details
   - Skills
   - LinkedIn/GitHub URLs
4. Review and save!

*Note: Resume parsing requires Perplexity API key (AI Settings tab)*
